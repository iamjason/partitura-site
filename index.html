ONE RECORDING. SIX PARTS.

Drums · Guitar · Bass · Piano · Vocals · Other

01 / LISTEN

Hear the little things.

Separate a song into instruments and listen to your part on its own. Come back to the original mix whenever you need the bigger picture.

02 / LEARN

Put notes to the sound.

Explore a transcription draft for each part. Make sense of the pitches and rhythm, then check the details with your own ears.

03 / KEEP

Take it to rehearsal.

Save isolated WAV audio for practice, MIDI for your music software, or MusicXML to edit the score. Your work stays yours.

LESS SETUP. MORE PLAYING.

Open a recording.
Follow your curiosity.

The audio engine comes with the app. Drop Partitura into Applications, open it, and bring a song—or record something of your own.

Apple silicon · macOS 14 or later · Clips up to 10 minutes and 120 MB

    Bring the sound.

    Open an audio file or allow microphone access to record.

  3. 3

A DRAFT, WITH ROOM FOR YOUR EARS

Music is in the details.

Automatic transcription is a starting point. Review pitches, rhythm, and bar alignment before playing from the score. The starting meter assumes 4/4; drum hits and guitar and piano separation are approximate.

A COMPANION IN THE MAKING

A little closer to your instrument.

The native iPhone and iPad companion is in development. It pairs with your Mac on the same private network using the Mac’s address and a pairing token. Processing happens on the Mac.

The public download is for Mac. TestFlight is not available yet.

✳

First use may download model weights. Leave several GB of free space for the models and your recordings. Audio is processed on your Mac, with no cloud transcription account.
